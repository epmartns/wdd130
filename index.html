Santa Catarina is a state in the south region of Brazil, located between the states of Rio Grande do Sul (south) and Paraná (north). At the west, Santa Catarina shares border with Argentina. The state is known by its beaches and festivals, such as the Oktoberfest, in Blumenau, the biggest German festival outside Germany.
